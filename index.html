01 VERIFIED INFORMATION

This mountain
actually exists.

Carrick Mountain rises directly above Glenealy in County Wicklow. The summit reaches 381 metres and is one of the eastern foothills of the Wicklow Mountains.

Most of the mountain is covered in forest, but large rocky knolls and granite outcrops appear around the summit. Forest roads climb surprisingly close to the top, with steep sections and hairpin bends along the way.

Historical records give earlier forms of the name as Carrigmurrely in 1756 and Carrickmacreily in 1795.
